## Project Title

The Impact of Crude Oil Price Fluctuations on UK Household Expenditure: A Comparative Analysis of Low and Middle-Income Families


## Project Overview

Crude oil price volatility directly affects UK fuel prices, which in turn influences household spending patterns across different income groups. Low and middle-income families are disproportionately impacted as they spend a higher proportion of their disposable income on essential items like transport, heating, and food—all of which are sensitive to energy costs.

## Objective

To build a machine learning pipeline that quantifies and predicts the differential impact of crude oil price changes on the spending behavior of low vs. middle-income UK households.

## Key Research Questions

    -- How do crude oil price changes translate to UK fuel price movements?

    -- What is the lag effect between crude oil price changes and household spending adjustments?

    -- How does this impact differ between low-income (bottom 20%) and middle-income (middle 40%) families?

    -- Which expenditure categories (transport, food, housing, utilities) are most sensitive to oil price shocks?

    -- Can we predict household spending changes based on oil price trends?

## Datasets Used

     * Brent Crude Oil Prices: 	            Investing.com / ONS(UK Office Of National Statistics)
     * UK Petrol & Diesel Prices:	        UK Government (DESNZ)
